Why growing sales don't mean growing profit

A three-page Power BI dashboard on a simulated FMCG distributor. Revenue was up 7.6% to $58M — but underneath it, three leaks were quietly eating the profit.

Empty shelves. December stockouts doubled; Masvingo ran dry 3× as often as Harare. $1.11M in sales never happened.
Discounts that cost more than they earn. $1.03M in promo spend returned $543K. 14 of 40 campaigns lost money.
The bestseller that barely pays. The top seller ($7.6M) kept only 11.7% as profit after cost to serve.

The company and figures are fictional, built for portfolio purposes. The pattern — and the fix — is real.
