about

I turn raw, messy data into decisions people can act on. I work across the whole journey: extracting data, building the pipelines that move and clean it, modelling it, and delivering a dashboard a manager can read in a minute.

I care more about the question than the chart. A good dashboard is one that changes what someone does on Monday — so I spend as much time on data quality and the "so what" as on the visuals.

Lately I've been deep in AI too: LLM-assisted extraction that turns unstructured text into clean records, FastAPI backends, and n8n automations that run on their own. I also founded TatsTech, where I've taught 30+ students, and I build Sheafly on the side.
